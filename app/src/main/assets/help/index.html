Transfers between your accounts

Transfers made before 8:00 p.m. ET on a business day post the same day. Transfers after the cut-off post on the next business day.

Bill pay

Electronic payments usually arrive in 1-2 business days. Check payments can take up to 5 business days.

Send money

Only send money to people you know and trust. Payments sent to an enrolled email or mobile number usually arrive within minutes.
